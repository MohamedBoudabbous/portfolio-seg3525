About Me

I am a software engineering student at the University of Ottawa with an interest in web development, user interfaces and artificial intelligence. I have also worked on technical projects related to graphs, algorithms and machine learning.

This portfolio was created as part of the SEG3525 course to explore the foundations of UI/UX design and visual communication. Through this project, I am progressively learning how to design more modern, structured and accessible interfaces using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript.

The portfolio evolves throughout the semester to present the different projects and prototypes completed as part of the course.
